Foldie Books

A calm reader for your books and comics

Adding books

Open the Library tab and tap +, then choose EPUB, CBZ, CBR or PDF files from the Files app. You can also open a book from another app and choose Foldie Books.

Reader settings

While reading, swipe up on the page or tap the Aa button. Fonts, text size, spacing, page width, background colour and page-turn style are all there. Pinch the page to change the text size quickly.

Syncing between devices

Foldie Books syncs your books, bookmarks and reading position through your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, on each device.

Foldable devices

Open your foldable and books are shown as a two-page spread. Half fold it and the reading controls move to one side of the fold.
