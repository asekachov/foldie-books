Privacy policy

Foldie Books · last updated October 26, 2026

Foldie Books is a reader for books and comics you add yourself. It is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Foldie Books has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Your books and reading data

  • Books and comics you import are stored on your device.
  • Your reading positions, bookmarks, highlights and reader settings are stored on your device.
  • If your device is signed in to iCloud, Foldie Books syncs your books, bookmarks and reading positions through your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. Apple handles this storage under its own privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

Foldie Books does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. It reads only the files you choose to import.

Children

Foldie Books does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
